About MLA Cafe

The story behind your favorite neighborhood hangout spot.

Our Journey

Founded with a passion for traditional flavors and authentic Indian hospitality, MLA Cafe has grown from a humble street-side tea stall into a beloved community hub. The name "MLA" represents the voice and assembly of local food lovers who gather daily to discuss life, politics, and everything in between over a hot cup of tea.

We pride ourselves on using locally sourced ingredients, authentic spices, and hygienic cooking standards to prepare dishes that remind you of home. Every recipe is crafted to serve perfection on your plate.
